About this template

Live: omnira.framer.website

Omnira is a dark futuristic portfolio built for designers, creative studios, and digital makers who want a bold and memorable online presence. It combines cinematic visuals, sharp typography, high-contrast layouts, smooth interactions, and sci-fi inspired interface details to create a premium portfolio experience.

The template includes essential pages such as Home, Works, About, Contact, project detail pages, and a custom 404 page. It is designed to showcase selected work, explain services, introduce your process, present testimonials, and guide visitors toward starting a project.

Omnira also includes a detailed design document that explains the visual direction, color styles, text styles, layout system, components, sections, and customization notes. This makes it easier to understand the structure of the template and adjust it for your own brand or portfolio.
